## Platform notes
- **Instagram:** feed + story. Link in bio (stories: link sticker). 5-8 hashtags.
- **Facebook:** link ad uses `*_link-1200x628.png`. 1-3 hashtags.
- **LinkedIn:** link size or feed square. Lead with the business pain (health-check, services). 3-5 hashtags.
- **X:** link size. One hashtag max.
- **WhatsApp Status / TikTok:** story size and the story motion ad.
